# Askuala

A free college planner. Upload a syllabus. Get a calendar, notes, reminders, and Askuala Buddy.

Open it here: https://askualastudy.vercel.app

Sign in, add your courses, and connect Google if you want your class calendar and Drive.

## What you get

* Class times, homework, and readings from a syllabus
* What is due in the next 48 hours
* Big assignments in the next two weeks
* Work listed by week
* One email for tomorrow, and one on Sunday for the week ahead
* Buddy, who can update your planner when you ask

Each person has their own login. Classmates do not see your courses.
